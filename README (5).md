# While She Studied Me, I Studied Her

## CPT, Dr. S. Kumar, and a Reciprocal Study of Institutional Life

This repository houses my Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) materials, correspondence between me and Dr. S. Kumar, related records, and my ongoing analysis of that relationship through the same four-dimensional framework that I formally articulated in 2019:

> **political, social, religious, and economic reasons**

This repository is not intended to present CPT as the beginning of my inquiry. It was not.

By the time I entered CPT, I had already spent years deliberately observing institutions, the people who operate within them, and the difference between what institutions say they are and how they actually function in practice.

In 2019, I formally changed my name to **Iain Melchizedek** through a Louisiana petition that expressly identified **political, social, religious, and economic reasons** for the change. That act marked an already-developing transformation in how I understood authority, identity, institutions, religion, politics, society, economics, and my own relationship to them.

I assert that I have operated from the **Order of Melchizedek** since 2019.

Later that year, I deliberately re-entered the walls of the United States Government. I did not return naively. I returned paying close attention.

My education, professional experience, and prior federal service gave me multiple lenses through which to observe what I encountered: legal, political, sociological, technical, administrative, financial, and eventually psychological.

---

## What This Repository Documents

This repository documents one specific part of that larger inquiry: my experience in CPT with Dr. S. Kumar while I was simultaneously living through an active federal investigation arising from what the United States Government characterized as a CFPB data breach.

I reject that characterization as false.

At the same time that I was being investigated, pressured, and repeatedly confronted with an institutional account of events that I did not accept, I was also receiving psychological treatment from a clinician employed within another federal institution: the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

That overlap matters.

I was not merely processing trauma in therapy.

I was documenting the experience of being subjected to institutional pressure while also documenting how a federal clinician interpreted me, interpreted the events I brought into the room, and responded to the political, social, religious, and economic realities that surrounded my experience.

Dr. Kumar was assigned to examine my cognition and trauma through psychology.

I was simultaneously observing her.

The resulting archive is therefore reciprocal.

It preserves both:

- how I described and processed what was happening to me; and
- how Dr. Kumar, situated inside a federal institution, perceived, interpreted, questioned, supported, limited, redirected, or responded to what I brought into the therapeutic relationship.

## The Central Thesis

> **My CPT archive documents a reciprocal study conducted during one of the most consequential institutional conflicts of my life. Dr. Kumar was assigned to examine my cognition and trauma through psychology; I, already years into a deliberate political, social, religious, and economic inquiry into American institutions and the people who operate them, was simultaneously observing her. The resulting record preserves how each of us interpreted the other—and allows me now to examine how her location within those four dimensions shaped the help she offered, the limitations she displayed, and the harm I experienced within that relationship.**

A shorter formulation is:

> **While she studied me psychologically, I studied her politically, socially, religiously, and economically.**

The important point is that this observation did not begin inside CPT.

CPT became one field site within an inquiry already underway.

## The Four-Dimensional Inquiry

My analysis of Dr. Kumar is organized through the same four dimensions I formally identified in 2019.

### Political

I examine what she appeared to understand about political power, institutional authority, federal systems, public officials, and the political environment surrounding the events I was bringing into therapy.

I also examine what became visible when the political context of my experience exceeded the clinician's knowledge or the boundaries of what she believed could properly be discussed in therapy.

### Social

I examine professional socialization, class position, institutional status, education, hierarchy, role expectations, cultural assumptions, and the social environment in which a federal clinician practices.

I am interested in how those factors may shape what a professional recognizes, normalizes, questions, or fails to see.

### Religious

My spiritual authority did not originate in CPT, psychiatry, psychology, or the United States Government.

I entered this period already operating from the **Order of Melchizedek** and from a spiritual framework that I understood as distinct from institutional authority.

This repository therefore examines what happened when a psychological framework encountered a person who did not treat psychology as the highest authority for interpreting reality.

Later CPT materials make this especially visible in my distinction between psychological interpretation and spiritual interpretation.

### Economic

I examine the economic position of the professional within the institution: employment, compensation, career trajectory, professional incentives, advancement, institutional dependence, and the material structures surrounding professional life.

This dimension is not reducible to individual motive. It asks how economic location may shape institutional participation, professional boundaries, and the conditions under which a person works.

## Archival Principle

The record should preserve chronology.

For correspondence, filenames should begin with the date and time whenever known.

Example:

```text
2024-07-23_1240_CFPB-OIG-Law-Enforcement-Case_Psychological-Distress_Dr-Kumar.pdf
```

For multi-message threads:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_to_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_Subject_Dr-Kumar-Thread.pdf
```

The purpose of this convention is to make the archive readable as a timeline without requiring an external database.

## Evidence, Memory, and Analysis

This repository contains different kinds of material, and they should remain distinguishable.

### Contemporaneous records

Documents created or transmitted during the period itself, including emails, VA messages, worksheets, letters, and attachments.

### Personal recollection

My memory of conversations, sessions, disclosures, reactions, and events that may not have been fully captured in written records.

### Analysis

My present interpretation of those records and recollections through political, social, religious, economic, psychological, legal, technical, and spiritual lenses.

These categories may interact, but they are not identical.

Preserving those distinctions allows the archive to show not only what I think now, but what I knew, wrote, reported, and documented at the time.
